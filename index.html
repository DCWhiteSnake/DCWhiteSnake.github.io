Account Mapper

Partner platform for a bank, live in production

I designed and built the platform end to end, and led the stakeholder meetings that shaped it. Partner institutions send account records to the bank by API or SFTP and look up account names, and bank staff and partners each get their own portal.

  • Took it from requirements to production, working directly with the bank's stakeholders to turn their needs into a system partners could actually onboard onto.
  • Architected it as five services behind one gateway: authentication, partner onboarding, record ingestion, name enquiry and reporting.
  • Made it safe to open up to outside institutions: no partner can reach another's data, sessions can be revoked, and logins use two factors.
  • Large uploads never block anyone. Bulk and SFTP ingestion run in the background, in parallel per partner.

Omnichannel corporate banking

Corporate internet banking for a bank

Onboarding, approvals, limits, beneficiaries, cards and cheques for corporate customers and bank staff, in a 17-module .NET monolith.

innkorp.com ↗

A KYC vault for small businesses

innKorp gets Nigerian small businesses' KYC paperwork done, keeps it in a vault they own, and shares it with fintechs only with their consent. I lead the technology and write much of the code.

  • Fintechs plug in through a documented partner API, an embeddable widget and signed webhooks.
  • Business data stays private by design. Postgres denies access by default, and every read goes through the server.
  • Business names are checked against the Corporate Affairs Commission's rules before anyone files.

docs are a feature

Swagger tells you the shape.
A guide tells you the story.

A list of endpoints won't tell a mobile developer which call comes first, or what to do when an account locks. So on the omnichannel platform, each API serves a hand-written guide next to its Swagger page, with the flows, sample requests and errors you'll actually hit. The guide changes in the same pull request as the code, so it never drifts, and every build syncs the team's Postman collection.

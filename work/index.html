Four things I've built.

For banks, health insurers and small businesses in Nigeria, Liberia and Tanzania.

  1. Liberia · at Inlaks

    Account Mapper

    Banking infrastructure for Liberian banks: they plug in to share account records and confirm who owns an account before money moves. I designed and built it, and worked it out with the banks in the room.

    10+ partner institutions live

  2. Tanzania · at Inlaks

    Corporate banking

    Internet banking for a bank's business customers, plus a back office for staff. I work on the architecture alongside the lead backend engineer.

    Live on web and mobile

  3. Nigeria · at Inlaks

    HMO management platform

    The platform a Nigerian health insurer (HMO) runs on, from enrolling members to vetting hospital claims. I lead the backend.

    100k+ enrollees onboarded

  4. Nigeria · my company

    innKorp

    A KYC vault that small businesses own. They do the paperwork once and share it with fintechs on their own terms. I co-founded it and run the tech.

    In pilot with its first users

Mostly C# and .NET, with SQL Server, Postgres, Redis and Docker, and TypeScript when the job needs it. Want the details? Read the CV.
